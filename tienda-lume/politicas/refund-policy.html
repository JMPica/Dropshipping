30-day money-back guarantee

We want you to love your Lume ritual. If you're not happy with your purchase, you can request a return within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price.

How to start a return

  1. Contact us through our contact page or at jmpicaortiz1011@gmail.com with your order number and the item you'd like to return.
  2. We'll reply within one business day with the return instructions and address. Items sent back without first contacting us can't be accepted.
  3. Once we receive and check the item, we'll issue your refund to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Conditions

  • Items must be returned in their original packaging, complete with all accessories, and in a clean, resellable condition.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened silicone patches can only be returned if they are defective.
  • Return shipping costs are paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo or short video of the issue. We'll send you a free replacement or a full refund, and we'll cover any return shipping. In many cases you won't even need to send the item back.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order free of charge before it ships. Contact us as soon as possible; once an order has shipped, the return process above applies.

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different item is to return the one you have and place a new order.
