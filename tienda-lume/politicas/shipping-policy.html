Free tracked shipping to the United States

Standard shipping is free on every order shipped to the United States, with no minimum purchase. Every order includes a tracking number.

Processing time

We process orders within 1–3 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding US holidays). You will receive an email with your tracking number as soon as your order ships.

Delivery time

Delivery usually takes 7–12 business days after your order ships. Depending on the item, orders ship from our fulfillment partners' warehouses in the United States or overseas, so items from the same order may arrive in separate packages. Delivery to Alaska and Hawaii may take a few extra days.

Where we ship

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We can't ship to PO Boxes or APO/FPO/DPO addresses. Please double-check your shipping address at checkout: we can't be responsible for packages sent to an incorrect address provided by the customer.

Duties and taxes

Any import duties are covered by us, so you won't be asked to pay anything extra on delivery. Sales tax, where applicable, is calculated at checkout.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking hasn't updated for 7 days, or your order hasn't arrived within 20 business days, contact us and we'll investigate with the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost, we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund.

Contact

Questions about your order? Write to us through our contact page or at jmpicaortiz1011@gmail.com and we'll reply within one business day.
